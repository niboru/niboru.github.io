1. Body & Somatics (18 items)

Sore Throat / Cold Drafts

Sore throat caused by wind/cold hitting collar bone/chest and dry nasal mucosa. (!) Wear a mask and a hoodie. Cover your neck and chest.

Stomachache Recovery Protocol

After having a stomachache, stand and sway your body gently left and right to help recovery.

Do not lay down; it will make everything worse.

Tension Headache Protocol

Tension headache: keep head straight and look to the top left; massage your whole head, chin, temple; press pressure points on your shoulder and the back of neck. Relax tongue.

Root Cause: Caused by playing smartphone while lying down.

Diving Reflex & Vagus Nerve Stimulation (Anxiety/Tension)

The diving reflex is a natural response in humans and other mammals that helps them conserve oxygen while underwater. Give it a try when you feel a little more anxious than usual by dipping/splashing your face into/with cold water.

Humming or sighing: let tensions go by stimulating parasympathetic nervous system.

Heat Flush / Rapid Cooling

Pour lukewarm or cold water on the back of your neck if you feel warm for instant cooling.

In an enclosed space you feel warm/hot because the air is stagnant.

Sugar blood spike can make you feel warm.

Swelling, Pain & Injury Compresses

Cold compress for swollen/pain, and after that hot compress to boost recovery.

Postural Mechanics & Baseline Rule

Body holds the posture that you're used to/familiar with whether it's good/bad posture.

Stand / sit properly: push your hip forward a bit. This straightens your back for easier breathing too. Happens when you sit a lot.

Exercise Volume, Pain & Rest Days

When exercising: stop when you feel slight pain, stop when you feel tired and cannot maintain your good posture.

  • Light exercise: rest for 1 day
  • Medium exercise: rest for 2 days
  • After you got your strength, maintain it by reducing your exercise a bit.
Proper Push-up Form

Do the right push up: wrist and both legs slightly open, back straight, armpit close, shoulder in front of supporting hand.

Breathing & Feldenkrais Muscle Reset

Do a rib cage breathing if tension is too high even for belly breathing.

When you are stiff or over-using your muscle, do a feldenkrais movement to reintroduce how to use muscles properly.

Frequent Urination & Full Electrolyte Depletion Protocol

Frequent urination can wash out sodium fastest, magnesium moderately, calcium least; re-supplement body calcium, magnesium, and sodium.

(Easy daily resupply)
Water 1 L + salt 1/4 tsp (≈500 mg sodium) sip slowly;
Food sources:
- Electrolytes + water: bananas, oranges, tomatoes
- Calcium (Ca): yogurt, milk, sardines, tofu
- Magnesium (Mg): pumpkin seeds, nuts, beans, greens
Optional supplements:
- Mg glycinate or citrate: 200–300 mg elemental/day
- Ca pill: only if intake < 800 mg/day (prefer calcium citrate 300–500 mg/day split doses)

(One-shot DIY electrolyte drink)
1 L water + 1/4 tsp salt + 1/2 tsp sugar or honey + squeeze lemon/orange; no heavy need for Ca in drinks.

(Edge cases)
- Heavy sweat / exercise: add another 1/4 tsp salt per sweaty hour
- Hot climate / fever: same
- Diarrhea / vomiting: OralRehydrationPackets preferred (Oralit)
Drinking Technique & Hydration Retention

Avoid drinking water quickly (gulping down); take sip by sip slowly is much better.

Avoid cold water; avoid drinking when eating (do it after or before). Don't skip before bed. Too much is not good for kidney.

To reduce urination frequency: take water with salt and honey or something sweet (Cola)? This prevents the body from quickly discharging water.

Medicine & Vitamin Spacing

Take medicine/vitamin one hour in between.

Diet: MSG (Monosodium Glutamate)

MSG is ok, accompanying food that go with it problematic. MSG is a flavor enhancer, so the quality of the food that use it is quite low.

Diet: Egg Quality vs Labels

Benefit from eggs depends more on feed quality and farm hygiene than the "free-range" label itself.

Diet: Milo & Seasonal Foods

On rainy season, drink warm water or soup to avoid sluggishness.

Milo is warm, not good taken for a long time. Can clash with soymilk.

Calluses Treatment

Use aloe vera gel for calluses.

Mental Fatigue & Grounding Mechanics

Reduce screen time, rest occasionally, write notes to reduce brain load, feel grateful, grounding time.

Don't get too focused on something; it'll drain you.

Clean bed is better for sleeping.

Step on soil to get better psychologically.

2. Mindset, Stress & Principles (11 items)

Neutral Attention (Inward vs Outward Focus)

Don't focus your mind too much outwardly or inwardly. Inward focus creates and maintains mental rumination and will get worse if your body is already not feeling good. Meanwhile focusing outward takes more toll on your mind for the extra processing needed from your senses.

The best way is to be right in the middle: move gently and feel the movement.

Soft Attention Movement Guidance

Move with soft attention, neither monitoring yourself nor the object, just feeling the motion and letting it happen. Use sensation as guidance, not control. Gentle and stable move. Soft fingers, wrist, gaze, and knee.

Stopping Rumination & Memory Mechanics

To avoid rumination, do not attach sensation/feeling/conversation to a story, especially with "what if" and "when".

Human body cannot differentiate between a real event and the memory of the event.

Baseline Stress Management

Base line stress has to be low enough so when you feel physical discomfort you can just ignore and focus on doing other things instead of focusing on the uncomfortableness which makes everything worse.

Execution: Lowest Viable Outcome & Pressure

Thinking about practicality and the need to do it will give pressure.

Whenever you want to do something: first decide roughly when to do it, set the lowest possible outcome that is still worth doing or how long to do it. This is done to reduce the accumulated tensions later on.

Integrity & Subtle Hypocrisy (Confucian / Stoic)

Hypocrisy: Subtle life rules are only enforced to others and not to yourself.

Confucian and Stoic ideas of inner integrity: virtue means harmony between what you expect of others and what you demand of yourself. Integrity is not about perfection but about consistency between principle and practice.

Immediate Boundaries (Rudeness)

If someone is rude, tell them right away nicely, don't wait and get mad. That way, everyone plays fair!

The Cobra Effect

Cobra Effect: A classic example of how well-meaning incentives can backfire, producing the exact opposite of the intended outcome.

Sensory Irritants / Upsetting Triggers

Upsetting things: smell of burning incense stick, smoke from smokers, dog/animal sound, pungent/acrid/smelly smell, loud noise.

Cognitive Re-framing

Re-Framing your thinking to look things from a different perspective and feel better.

Product Marketing & Perception Filter

When buying something, focus on whether it's easy to maintain.

Standard of product pricing in Indo: below standard value is marketed as standard price, thus giving perception it's the standard value; meanwhile standard value is marketed as premium price and slap a nice logo on top of that.

Products on the market are made to attract you with cuteness, nice looking design, or simple things that's supposedly to help with daily life. But these products are made not to last long mostly or too prematurely made.

3. Home Maintenance & Cleaning Chemistry (10 items)

Toilet Bowl Mineral Ring (Citric Acid)

Clean yellowish hard mineral stain on toilet bowl using citric acid; dump around 4-5 spoons directly to the water bowl; wait for 30 to 60 mins and brush with non-scratching brush.

Buckets & Scaled Water Flasks (Citric Acid)

Clean dirty buckets or any water containers using citric acid and a little bit of water. Apply it then wait 15 mins. Your dirty buckets will all be cleaned magically.

White small debris in glass flasks: That can only be seen with bright light after pouring water from glass flask is calcium and magnesium carbonate naturally occurring from hard water. It's safe but better clean your flask thoroughly with citric acid + brush.

Kitchenware Cleaning (Calcium Carbonate & Toothpaste)

Clean kitchenware using calcium carbonate cream.

Toothpaste works as a universal safe cleaner, but leaves residue.

Odor Removal & Acid Neutralization (Baking Soda)

Remove odor or neutralize acid; use baking soda.

Electrical Contact Oxidation

Clean oxidation with 99% alcohol and a brush; optionally use dielectric grease to prevent re-oxidation especially if using contact cleaner.

Eraser and Fiberglass brushes are also possible. Oxidation is apparent near sea environment; blackening pins.

Cleaning Cloths & Scrubbers

Use green scrub on stubborn stains.

Use microfiber for cleaning cloth.

Teak Utensil Care (Beeswax vs Mineral Oil vs Varnish)

Teak spatula finishing:

  • Varnish (glossy shiny) - Avoid toxic varnish
  • Beeswax (matte, smooth) - Beeswax is the best
  • Mineral oil (deepens color) - Combines well with beeswax
Avocado Ripeness Test

Good avocado: Press twice on the same spot; both occasions should be soft/tender; need very little energy to cut.

Storage Boxes & Small Part Management

Use transparent box and thinwall for small storage.

Toilet Trap / Dry Bowl Gas Hazard

Do not dry toilet bowl more than 5 mins; caution the smell and dangerous gas.

3M VHB Adhesive Hooks: Load Ratings & Rules

Surface requirements: Needs smooth, non-porous clean surface (glass/ceramic); clean with alcohol; use 3M VHB tape.

Installation: Press hard for 10 seconds. Multiple adhesive pads give higher total load. Usually fails in the peel direction (the hook pulling away). Avoid painted walls (3M Primers can help). Avoid constant temperature change.

Coastal Living Storage & Air Coolers

Portable Air Cooler: If you live near sea, do not buy portable air cooler. It is useless when the humidity is already high.

Coastal Storage: Every stuff needs to be put inside sealed storage boxes with silica gel. Replace silica gel every 1-2 years.

Plastics off-gassing: Plastics leave odour and exhume VOC in closed containers; thus melting. Need to air it occasionally.

Battery Selection Matrix & Phone Charging

Cheap batteries (Carbon-Zinc) for: TV/AC remote, simple wall clock, cheap calculator, basic LED lights, toys without motor.

For everything else: Use alkaline or rechargeable battery.

Battery Charging Rule: Fast charging doesn't ruin battery; heat is. Charge 20–80%.

Android Phone Architecture Priority

Android phone: Idle management is the most important thing; little cores are important because they are used for general tasks.

Legal to F4 Paper Scaling

When you change page size from legal to f4, you practically scale it down, no matter using whatever software. So just simply scale to 85% when printing.

Clearing Printer Jams

Slip a thick paper from the bottom side of your printer to push debris out. Careful with the sensor.

Thermos Bottle Thermodynamics (Glass vs Stainless)

Stainless steel thermos cannot preserve heat long with little volume compared with glass thermos.

Airflow Dynamics (Narrow Passages & Stagnation)

Air will flow faster the narrower the passage is (door/hall, or whatever).

In an enclosed space you feel warm/hot because the air is stagnant.

Apartment Floor Selection Trade-Offs
  • Ladder reach up to 6–7 floors
  • Smoke is worse at top floors
  • Easy stair access on lower floors
  • Less sway on lower floors (less motion sickness)
  • Mid floors are cooler
  • Top floors windier
  • Less time elevator waiting on lower floors
  • Higher floors more dusty in windy environment
  • Lower floors more humid and noisy
